Smart cube PCI

Scramble the cube with the turns shown, then solve it against the clock. Connect a GiiKER Bluetooth cube (Chrome or Edge on a desktop or Android), or turn the on-screen cube: drag a face, or press U R F D L B (Shift for counterclockwise). This is the QTI 3 portable custom interaction from tspci-smart-cube, running on its own in this page.
